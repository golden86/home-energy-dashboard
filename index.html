Season boundaries and switching assumptions

Winter is December–February, spring March–May, summer June–August and autumn September–November. The two partial Novembers in the meter year combine in the autumn and extended-winter figures. An extended winter combines the relevant calendar months from across the sample.

Historical Agile is matched to each half hour. The alternative uses the quoted or published November 2026 rates throughout. The replay keeps your recorded timing, includes each standing-charge day once, and assumes the switch and return happen at the window boundaries. Seasonal totals exclude incentives, extra smart-charging sessions and any interim supplier tariff.

A winter-only plan incurs the listed electricity exit fee once, assuming an early return to Agile. Edit the fee in Comparison if the supplier confirms a waiver. Availability, transfer dates and future prices can differ. Octopus currently requires 30 days on one smart tariff before moving to another. After moving from a smart tariff to a standard tariff, there is a 30-day wait to rejoin a smart tariff. Octopus smart tariff terms ↗
